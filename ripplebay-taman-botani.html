Ripplebay Taman Botani

A serene waterfront haven nestled inside the historic Perdana Botanical Garden. Perfect for healing, tranquil canopy electric boat cruises, swan pedal boats, and lush tropical greenery.

Visiting Tips & Buggy Transportation
  • Shuttle Buggy (RM 5/pax): Hop on our convenient buggy ride straight from the main garden entry to Ripplebay jetty.
  • Cruise Boat: Canopied electric boat ride sheltered from sun and gentle rain.
  • Botanical Flora: Enjoy panoramic views of heritage trees, water lilies, and birdlife.
  • Swan Pedal Boats: Glide across the calm waters at your own gentle rhythm.
  • Safety First: Certified life jackets provided and compulsory for all riders.
