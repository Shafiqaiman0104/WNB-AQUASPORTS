The 6 Signature Aquatic Challenges

Real action, real laughter, and genuine team bonding in open water. Challenge #01 (Water Confident) is compulsory for open-water acclimatization and safety, followed by your squad's choice of 2 or more challenges below:

Challenge 01
Acclimatization & Trust

Water Confident

Participants submerge into the serene lake water safely supported by certified buoyancy life vests. Guided by certified lifeguards, this foundational drill melts water anxiety, builds self-assurance, and establishes team trust in deep open water.

Compulsory Foundation
Fear Conquering Water Safety Team Trust
Challenge 02
Raw Power & Determination

Kayak Tug O' War

Two double kayaks face opposite directions locked together by a heavy marine rope. At the starting horn, crews paddle furiously forward in unison to drag the opposing boat past the marker buoy. A high-energy spectacle of strength and coordination!

Explosive Power Stroke Rhythm High Spirit
Challenge 03
Agility & Swift Retrieval

The Golden Duck Scramble

Scores of golden floating ducks are scattered across the lake. Teams scramble and steer through tricky angles, coordinating boat navigation and reach to retrieve as many ducks as possible before the clock expires. Sharp eyes and quick turns dominate!

Target Agility Maneuvering Quick Reflexes
Challenge 04
Pure Speed & Cadence

Lakeside Sprint Regatta

An electrifying straight-line sprint from buoy Point A to Point B with the majestic KL skyline in the backdrop. Kayakers must match paddle depth, cadence, and boat balance to reach maximum hull velocity and clinch first place on the winners podium.

Head-to-Head Sprint Synchronicity Podium Glory
Challenge 05
Creative Propulsion & Fun

No-Paddle Hand Scramble

All paddles are removed! Crews must propel their boats toward the marker using only bare hands, body motion, splashing teamwork, or innovative propulsion techniques. Hilarious, engaging, and demanding of creative collaborative spirit.

Unfiltered Fun Bare-Hand Drive Adaptive Thinking
Challenge 06
Secret Relay & Memory

Broken Radio Secret Relay

One paddler memorizes a secret phrase at the command dock, races across open water, and whispers it verbally into their teammate's ear kayak-to-kayak (no writing allowed!). The final paddler races back to transcribe it. The most accurate transcription wins!

Active Listening Memory Pressure Relay Delivery
