Select Your 3 Challenge Games

Game 1 (Water Confident) is compulsory for safety and acclimatization, followed by 2 games of your choice:

Challenge #01

Water Confident

Each person will swim on the lake using only a safety life jacket. This essential activity builds personal self-confidence, eliminates water anxiety, and teaches participants to become braver, especially in deep open water.

Compulsory
Challenge #02

Kayak Tug O' War

Two canoes face opposite directions (2 or 4 people per canoe) tied together with a strong heavy rope. Teams paddle intensely forward to pull the rope to their side. The team that secures the longest rope wins!

Challenge #03

The Golden Duck

Teams scramble across the lake in their canoes to retrieve floating golden ducks scattered across designated zones. Rapid navigation and sharp eyes are crucial. The team collecting the most golden ducks claims victory.

Challenge #04

Kayak Race

High-intensity sprint! Each squad paddles their canoe in unison from Point A to Point B. The first team with all racers successfully crossing the finish line leg of the race takes the winner's podium.

Challenge #05

No Paddle Kayak Race

Paddles are removed! Teams must move their kayaks from Point A to Point B solely relying on bare hands, body motion, teamwork, or any creative propulsion strategy. Highly hilarious and demanding of true team synchronization!

Challenge #06

Broken Radio

One player draws a secret phrase from a bag and memorizes it. They paddle to Point B and relay the phrase verbally to Team B — strictly no writing allowed! Team B then paddles back to write what they heard. Closest phrase wins points!

Your Safety Is Our Top Priority

We adhere to rigorous marine safety protocols to ensure peace of mind for you and your family.

Mandatory Life Jackets

Every participant, regardless of swimming ability, must wear an approved, properly fitted life jacket before boarding.

Trained Marshals On Duty

Certified water safety marshals and rescue motorboats actively patrol the lake surface during all operational hours.

Weather Monitored

Operations are temporarily halted in the event of heavy rain or lightning warnings for the absolute safety of riders.

Attire Recommendations

Comfortable light sportswear or quick-dry apparel is recommended. A change of clothes is advised for bumper boat rides!

However, advance reservations are required for corporate team building and private group bookings.
